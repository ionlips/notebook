21:13:08: Modified the files under `dot_config/shell` to use braces and quotes
only when necessary. Here is the explanation from Claude:

````markdown
I removed the braces and quotes that weren't needed in both files and kept the ones that are. I sourced both files in bash and zsh afterwards: `PATH`, `MANPATH` and `HISTFILE` come out correctly, and so do the aliases and functions. Nothing is applied or committed yet.

**What I removed**
- **`env.sh.tmpl` (`prepend`):** the quotes and braces in `eval list=\$$1`, `eval $1=\$2\${list:+:\$list}` and `export $1`, and the quotes on the plain assignments such as `list=:$list:`. Assignments are never split, and those `eval` lines only ever build an assignment. I tested `prepend` on its own in both shells with entries containing spaces and `*`, and it behaved correctly.
- **`interactive.sh`:**
  - The braces in `${ZSH_VERSION-}` and `${BASH_VERSION-}`. The `-` only matters with `set -u`, which you don't use.
  - The quotes around `$XDG_STATE_HOME/zsh` and `$NVM_DIR/nvm.sh`. This matches how you already write `$HOME` paths.
  - The quotes around the `echo` message. `echo` joins its arguments with single spaces, so the output is the same.

**What I kept, because it's needed**
- **Braces in `${list//…}`, `${list#:}`, `${list%:}` and `${list:+…}`:** this kind of substitution only works with braces.
- **`"$list" != "${list//:$2:/:}"`:** bash splits unquoted variables in `[ … ]`, and these values come from the inherited `PATH`, `MANPATH` and `INFOPATH`, which you don't control.
- **`[ -n "$TMUX" ]` and `[ -n "$ZSH_VERSION" ]`:** if the variable is empty, the unquoted test becomes `[ -n ]`, which is always true.
- **`"$@"`:** keeps each argument intact.
- **`"#S"`:** without quotes, `#` starts a comment.
- **`eval "$(starship init …)"`:** without quotes, the script's line breaks get flattened and glob characters can expand.
- **The alias values:** they contain spaces or `&&`.

The `wget` alias on line 12 of `interactive.sh` is now redundant with the `~/.local/bin/wget` wrapper. Do you want me to remove it?
````
