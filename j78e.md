# Copy commit from one branch to another

I updated the binary used for Tree-sitter in my dotfiles and made a commit to
main. I needed this change to be applied to the linux/non-root branch too.
Within lazygit, this can be done as follows:

1.  Open lazygit, and switch to linux/non-root.
1.  In the same pane, click `enter` on main - this will bring up all of its
    commits in the same pane.
1.  Hover over the commit you want to copy and press `C`.
1.  Navigate to the pane below (commits on the current branch) and press `V`.
    This will ask you if you want to paste the copied commit.
