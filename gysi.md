# YouTube songs on Spotify using yt-dlp

## Download on macOS and transfer to iOS

1.  Download the song on your macOS device.
1.  Plug your iOS device into your macOS device.
1.  Navigate to Finder > iOS device (i.e., iPhone 17e) > Files > Spotify and
    copy the file to this directory.
1.  Open Spotify and ensure Settings and privacy > Apps and devices > Local
    audio files is enabled.
1.  The song should be available within your Local Files playlist.
