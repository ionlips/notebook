# Introduction to EuroHPC AI Factories access modes: Overview, evaluation process, and success stories

Recording will be available on EuroHPC's YouTube channel.

Evangelos Floros is the Head of Unit Infrastructure.

AI Factory Antennas (AIFAs) give access to those countries who do not want to
invest in the hardware.

Specific AI Factories (AIFs) focus on specific sectors (i.e., robotics,
aerospace, etc.) - you can check EuroHPC for this information.

Focus is on SMEs and start-ups.

First systems should be online during Q1 of 2027.

AI Gigafactories (AIGFs) is an initiative where EuroHPC will try buy from
European "hyperscalars" to obtain infrastructure for AI-related work.

Annika Kossack is the Project Coordination Officer for the peer review process.

Valid VAT number is required for access to AIFs through the industrial channel.

Technical assessment is the only thing done for Fast Lane and Playground.

EU-funded projects have priority in the scientific channel.

EIC accelerator awardees have priority for Large Scale.

SMEs can apply for both the industrial and scientific channel?

```text
Q: I can see that SMEs applied to the scientific channel. What is the difference in applying to the industrial vs. scientific channel for SMEs and industry?
A: This access mode covers all types of scientific users, users from public sector, as well as industrial users participating in R&I projects. All other types of industrial users should target the AI for Industry tracks.
```
