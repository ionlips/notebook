# AIF IRL-Antenna: Group working model proposal

## Summary

This is a proposal for how Abin Madanchery, Ion Lipsiuc, and James Grogan will
work together on the AIF IRL-Antenna programme and how we will interact with
colleagues on the programme.

## What is "the group"?

This is an AI sandbox development group - a group of developers working on an
element of the AIF IRL-Antenna programme.

## How will the group operate?

-   Where will group documents go?
-   How will group planning be tracked?
-   How will the group communicate?

## What is the group's understanding of the AIF IRL-Antenna's goals?

-   Summary of project proposal.
-   Statement of understanding of current project goals and timeline.
-   Who else is working on the AIF IRL-Antenna; what are they focusing on?
-   How does it interact with AI, HPC, ICHEC, and (inter)national SMEs?

## What is the short/medium-term plan for the group?

-   Set up group working model and practices:
    -   Shared documentation.
    -   Communication channel(s).
    -   Issue tracking and planning.
-   Solidify understanding of project requirements and stakeholders.
-   Domain upskilling and knowledge transfer.
-   Initial skills for AI agents.
-   Anticipate MeluXina interaction and required technology:
    -   MCP server.
    -   How does the environment being a K8s cluster change things?
